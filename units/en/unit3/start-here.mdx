# Start here: this week

Week 3 runs **four one-hour classes, Monday to Thursday**, and the capstone presentation on
**Friday 2 October**. Everything you need this week is in this unit. You should not have to
go back to unit 0 or unit 2.

## Two things to finish, and they are separate

| | The final assignment | The Gecko capstone |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | your research agent, answering from six documents | your own store on devnet, and a buyer agent that buys from it through Gecko |
| How it is judged | privately, on a question set, for a signed certificate | the six-minute presentation on Friday |
| Where you build it | `my-final-assignment`, made with `uv run bootcamp final new ../my-final-assignment` | `my-gecko-buyer`, cloned from the capstone repository |
| Start with | [the final assignment tutorial](final-assignment.mdx) | [the Gecko capstone](gecko-capstone.mdx), then [step by step](capstone-tutorial.mdx) |

**What is graded, and how to score all of it:** [everything that is graded](graded-work.mdx):
the 15 marked items on the track, with a link to each, and the final assignment.

## Every day

1. **Before class:** `git pull`, then `uv sync --extra projects --extra agents`.
2. **In class:** open the session's follow-along; the table at its top says what fits in the hour.
3. **After class:** the follow-along's "Keep going on your own" section lists what to finish,
   and the capstone's project for the day is in its repository.
4. **Stuck?** Ask the course from your assistant (below). It answers from these pages and
   names the page, so you can check.
