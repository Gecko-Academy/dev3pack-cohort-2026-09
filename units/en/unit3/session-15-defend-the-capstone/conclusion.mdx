# Conclusion

You built a source-grounded assistant over three weeks, one abstraction at a
time, and then defended it while something was breaking.

## What you can now do

A short list, because each item is real:

- put a contract in front of a model and enforce it
- give an agent a tool that refuses
- run a loop that stops
- measure retrieval rather than trusting it
- attack your own evaluator
- keep a trace you can read under pressure
- say what would change your mind

## What this course did not make you

Someone who has run this in production for a year. The capstone is a bounded
prototype with its controls visible, which is a different and more honest claim
than "production ready".

## What comes next

The next steps are yours to pick, and the depth track, the cookbook and the
optional bonus units are all still here after the cohort ends. So is the
repository.
