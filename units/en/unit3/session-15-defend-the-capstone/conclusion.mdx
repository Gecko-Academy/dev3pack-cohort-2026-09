# Conclusion

You opened a store on Solana devnet, wrote a buyer that pins what was asked before any
bytes exist, and defended it while the judge tried to make it sign something else.

## What you can now do

A short list, because each item is real:

- read a program's accounts through Gecko instead of guessing them
- pin a request before anything can change it
- compare a prepared transaction with the request, field by field, as addresses and
  whole numbers
- sign only after every check agrees, with a key that never leaves your machine
- prove a purchase landed from two ledger reads, not from a success message
- serve your check as a small MCP server that refuses a private URL
- say what would change your mind

## What this course did not make you

Someone who has run a payment system in production for a year. The capstone is a bounded
prototype with its controls visible: one unit per purchase, three espressos on mainnet,
and a check that trusts your pin. That is a more honest claim than "production ready".

## What comes next

Your store stays on devnet, and your repository is yours. The depth track, the cookbook
and the optional bonus units are all still here after the cohort ends.
