# Depth track (optional)

Five self-paced modules on the software engineering underneath the agent work.
Optional, ungraded, and available from day one. Nothing in the fifteen sessions
waits for them.

They exist because the course teaches you to steer an assistant, and you cannot
steer toward a tradeoff you do not know exists. Each module makes you decide
something and then holds you to the decision.

| Module | The decision you make |
|---|---|
| [Full stack](../../../depth/01-full-stack/) | Where the boundary goes, and what a caller sees when it works, refuses or breaks |
| [Managing data](../../../depth/02-managing-data/) | In memory or durable, and what you refuse to keep |
| [Architecture](../../../depth/03-architecture/) | Synchronous or not, and the measurement that reverses the call |
| [Secure and reliable](../../../depth/04-secure-and-reliable/) | Fail closed or degrade, and the risk you accept |
| [Production](../../../depth/05-production/) | Measured or aspirational, and what you would page on |

Fourteen checked exercises, all offline against the deterministic model double.
No key, no cloud, no cost. Every module runs in CI on every push, so they do
not rot between cohorts.

They are not counted toward anything. Do them because the decisions in session
10 and the capstone get easier once you have made these ones on purpose.
