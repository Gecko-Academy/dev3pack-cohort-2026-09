# Handing work in

Every unit with a notebook can be handed in. Week 0 is a record of the work and
is never marked. Sessions are scored from what their checks print. Two sessions
are assistant-driven and are handed in rather than marked, and their pages say so.

```bash
uv run bootcamp check ch03                       # what passes, what does not
uv run bootcamp submit ch03 --github <you>       # writes submissions/<you>/ch03/
```

The bundle holds two files: `submission.json`, the claim, and `notebook.ipynb`,
the evidence. Copy the folder into your fork of the submissions repository and
open a pull request. CI checks the shape, that the folder is yours, that the
notebook is the file the claim was written against, and that the score follows
from the passes and help the claim lists. A human merges, and the merged file
is your recorded score.

Submitting again replaces the earlier attempt. Nothing is locked, and nothing is
lost: the history is the pull requests.
