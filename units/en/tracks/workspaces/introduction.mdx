# Workspaces

Four open-ended projects in the DataCamp shape: a brief, a few chapters, and
room to build. Ungraded, with no checks, because the point is a decision you
make rather than an answer you match.

| Workspace | What you build |
|---|---|
| [Week 1: corpus QA](../../../../workspaces/week-1-corpus-qa/) | Question answering over your own corpus |
| [Week 2: retrieval lab](../../../../workspaces/week-2-retrieval-lab/) | Retrieval you measure and then change |
| [Week 3: Let Me Buy](../../../../workspaces/week-3-let-me-buy/) | An agent against real on-chain rails, read-only |
| [News to Telegram](../../../../workspaces/news-to-telegram/) | A small pipeline end to end, with the reading checklist it opens with |

Use them when a session's lab left you wanting more room than the notebook had.
They execute in CI, so they stay runnable, but nothing here is submitted and
nothing is scored.
