# Data licence

`reviews.csv` is a 1,000-row sample of **Women's E-Commerce Clothing Reviews**,
published by Nick Brooks on Kaggle under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**:
<https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nicapotato/womens-ecommerce-clothing-reviews>

It is real commercial data. The publisher anonymised it and replaced references to
the company in the review text with "retailer". Nothing here identifies a person.

No other file in this project comes from a third party.
